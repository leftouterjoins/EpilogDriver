This installs two things for your Epilog Zing laser engraver.

Epilog Studio

The application. Open a PDF, SVG or image, see it laid out on a drawing of the bed, and decide what happens to each colour in it — cut, engrave, score, or nothing at all. Trace the outline with the beam off to line up your material before you burn anything. It sends jobs to the laser directly, with no printer queue involved.

The printer driver

For when a document is already laid out. Adds the Zing as a printer, so you can print to it from any application. Less control than the app, but sometimes you just want to hit Print.

Supported Models

  • Epilog Zing 16 - 16" x 12" engraving area
  • Epilog Zing 24 - 24" x 12" engraving area

Both give you

  • Raster engraving with adjustable power and speed
  • Vector cutting, with per-colour settings
  • Dithering, for photographs and gradients
  • 3D engraving (greyscale power modulation)
  • 100, 200, 250, 400, 500 and 1000 DPI
Note: Your Epilog Zing must be connected to the same network as your Mac, or directly via a crossover cable.
